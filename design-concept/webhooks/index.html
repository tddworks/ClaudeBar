The rules this shows

    
    
      
  • No percent per webhook: it sends the Quota alerts percentages, and status alerts if ticked.
    • 
      
  • A webhook wants an alert when it is on, sends that kind, and the alert's provider is in its scope.
    • 
      
  • The URL must be https://, except a host on this Mac or your network.
    • 
      
  • Send a test sends a message labelled as a test — never a made-up alert — and works on a webhook that is off.
    • 
      
  • One attempt per alert, no retry. The last delivery is shown: Sent, Failed — 404, Nothing sent yet.
    • 
      
  • The URL is kept in the Keychain (or, when the Keychain refuses, the app credential store, and the card says so) — never in settings.json or a log.
