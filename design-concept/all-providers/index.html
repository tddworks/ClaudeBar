What a card shows

The provider, its status chip, a ring for its tightest quota (the one that runs out first) and up to two quota rows with their resets. A provider with several accounts shows its tightest account and a count ("3 accounts"). One that isn't signed in or failed shows -- and the reason, so nothing silently disappears.

Order stays put

Cards follow the pill order you already set in Settings → Providers. They don't jump around as quotas change, so Riko's eyes learn where Claude lives.

Not in the overview

Cost, today's usage and the 30-day chart stay on each provider's page. The overview answers "can I keep working?", not "how much did I spend?".

Keyboard

⌘0 All · ⌘1–⌘9 a provider, as today · ← → move between cards, ↩ opens one.
