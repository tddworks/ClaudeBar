An image of what's on your card. Nothing is uploaded; the board's link is printed on it.

The preview is the image. What you see is exactly what gets copied or saved.

  • Shape: Square (1080×1080, for X, Instagram and chats) or Wide (1200×630, the link-card shape for READMEs and blogs).
  • Name: starts as the eye on Your rank has it. With the eye on, it starts masked, so screen-sharers stay safe.
  • Copy image is the main action: paste into X, Slack or Discord. Save… writes a PNG. Share… opens the Mac's share menu (Messages, AirDrop, Mail…).

No text field, no "post to X" button. ClaudeBar never signs in anywhere to post for you.
