    Where the country comes from
    Cloudflare's edge already knows which country a request comes from. The server stores that two-letter code for members who opted in. The Mac asks for no location, and the IP is never stored.
    
    
    What's public
    Per-country totals only: how many members and how many tokens. Never who is where. A VPN shows the VPN's country.
    
    
    The threshold
    A country appears once three opted-in members are there. One member alone in a country would be identifiable on a small board.
