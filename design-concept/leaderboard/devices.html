A device is a key

Every Mac or PC signs with its own key, kept on that machine only. Adding one is approving its code from a device you have; removing one revokes its key at once. Up to five in use.

Never approved blind

Before Approve, the Mac shows the label the new device asked with and when. The new device, once approved, names the member and asks “Is that you?” before it keeps or sends anything.

A first week

An added device can't add, remove, rename or leave for 7 days, and sees only itself. Removing another device still in its first week offers to delete the days it sent.

A copy asks first

A Mac whose key came from another Mac uploads nothing until the member answers: its own device, or keep the key here. The card says plainly what keeping it means while the other Mac is in use.
