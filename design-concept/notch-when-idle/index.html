Notch When Idle

What the notch does when nothing is happening becomes the person's choice (#627). Mock data only.

Settings → General

Notch Live Activity
Show session and quota state in the notch. Appears only while there is something to report.
When Nothing's Happening
Keep the quota in the notch, or hide it until a session works, waits for you or a quota runs low.
Touch Bar
Show persistent quota and status on the MacBook Touch Bar.

The notch

  • The row sits right under the notch switch and shows only while the notch is on.
  • Show the Quota is the default: nothing changes for anyone who doesn't pick.
  • Hide drops only the quota glance. Working, agents working, Needs you, Done and a quota under 20% still show.
  • Without session hooks (Codex, Gemini), Hide leaves only the low-quota state.
