# Quota pools: what your logins have left, together

Keep more than one login of a product ([multiple accounts](../multi-account/README.md)) and the popover adds them up for you. Under the **ACCOUNTS** chips, a small line says what they have left together:

**340% left across 3 logins**

Each login counts by the number it already shows — its tightest window's percent. Over 100% is normal and means what it says: two logins with room each make a bigger total, because every login refills on its own.

## What counts

- **Watched logins only.** A paused login is out of every judgement, the pool included — resume it and it counts again.
- **Seen numbers only.** A login that hasn't reported yet, or that shows only a prepaid balance (dollars, no percent), is left out rather than guessed. Its card still shows what it has.
- **Two or more.** The line shows once at least two logins bring a percent; one login's room is already on its own card.
- **One product at a time.** Codex's pool sums Codex's logins; Claude's never mixes in.

## What it is not

- **Not a forecast.** It says what is left now; it doesn't say how long that will last.
- **Not a percentage of anything bigger.** There is no plan or budget behind it — it is simply the sum across your logins.

*Switch when low* still works one login at a time: it moves new sessions to the login with the most left, whether the pool is high or low.
